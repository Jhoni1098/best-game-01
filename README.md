**Voidgarden** — a night-garden defense roguelite. The blight comes at night: wisps, skitters, spitters, splitters and brutes march on the Heartbloom at the garden's centre. You are the gardener — move with WASD, aim with the mouse, shoot bolts of light, and spend the seeds your kills drop on living blooms (Thornspitter, Frostbloom, Sunburst) that fight beside you. Clear a wave, pick one of three boons, and hold the garden for 15 waves until the Blight King falls. Lose the Heartbloom — or your gardener — and the night keeps the garden.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network: `game/` is plain static files, every sound synthesized live with WebAudio. Screenshots are deterministic scenes from `?shot=title|play|upgrade|over&seed=1` (see `shots.json`).
